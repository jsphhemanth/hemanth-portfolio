A Few Honest Words ♥

When I started here, I was quietly terrified. New to the systems, new to the team, and trying to earn the trust of people who already had their rhythm. You did not make me prove myself before you helped me. You just helped me. That is rare, and I will not forget it.

This is not a goodbye. This is a "see you around." My number is the same. My email is the same. And if any of you ever need anything, I mean that with zero conditions attached.

Thank you for making Wentworth Point feel less like a workplace and more like a second home.
